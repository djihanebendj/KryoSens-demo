# KB-CR-105: Compressor & Pressure Switch Fault Codes Reference

## Section 8.1: High/Low Pressostat Trip Indicators (Read-Only Diagnostics)
Controller alarm code E04 indicates that the low-pressure pressostat has tripped at 0.8 bar due to suction pressure drop or refrigerant starvation. Alarm code E05 indicates that the high-pressure pressostat has tripped at 24.0 bar due to condenser fouling or condenser fan failure. Technicians may read the controller alarm log and check condenser airflow, but must never alter protection setpoints without certified commissioning instruments.

## Section 8.2: Thermostatic Expansion Valve (TXV) Superheat Range
Normal evaporator exit superheat for chilled cold rooms should measure between 5.0 K and 8.0 K under steady compressor load. Hunting superheat oscillations exceeding 4.0 K amplitude indicate an oversized orifice or loose thermal bulb clamp on the suction line.
