# KB-CR-103: Evaporator Defrost & Airflow Diagnostics Guide

## Section 6.4: Electric & Hot-Gas Defrost Termination Parameters
Standard electric defrost cycles run every 6 hours for a maximum duration of 30 minutes and terminate when the evaporator coil termination sensor reaches 12.0 C. Post-defrost evaporator fan delay should be set to 3 minutes to allow coil drip-down and prevent warm moisture blow-off into the cold room. If pull-down recovery to 4.0 C takes longer than 45 minutes after defrost termination, inspect evaporator coil fin icing and fan blade airflow.

## Section 6.5: Evaporator Fan & Drain Line Verification
Evaporator fans must maintain uniform air throw across the storage volume with a coil temperature differential (TD) between 4.0 C and 6.0 C. Blocked condensate drain lines or failed drain-pan heater tapes cause ice build-up from the bottom of the evaporator shroud within 24 hours.
