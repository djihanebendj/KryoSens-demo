EX-200 TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Fictional product and invented values, written for ColdSense retrieval tests.
Not guidance for any real equipment.

1 Compressor does not start
Check the supply voltage at the terminal block. If the thermal overload has tripped, wait 15 minutes before
resetting it. If the compressor still does not start, check the start relay.

2 High room temperature with compressor running
Check the condenser coil for dirt, the evaporator for ice, the evaporator fans and the door gasket.

3 Evaporator icing
Check the defrost heater. Its resistance should be between 40 and 60 ohm. Check that the defrost termination
probe is fixed to the evaporator coil.

4 Error codes
E1 means a room probe fault. E2 means an evaporator probe fault. E3 means the high pressure switch has tripped.
