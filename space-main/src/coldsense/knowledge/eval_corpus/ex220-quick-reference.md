# EX-220 Reach-in Freezer Quick Reference

> SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Fictional manufacturer ("Exemplar Refrigeration", invented for ColdSense tests),
> fictional product and invented values. A near-match model name for the EX-200, used to test that documents
> for one model are never cited for another. Not guidance for any real equipment.

## Specifications

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Operating range | -22 to -16 °C |
| Refrigerant | R-290, charge 95 g |
| Supply | 230 V, 50 Hz |

## Defrost

The EX-220 uses hot-gas defrost. The defrost interval is 12 h and a defrost cycle lasts at most 20 min.

## Door alarm

The door alarm sounds when the door stays open for more than 2 min.

## Fan delay

After a defrost, the evaporator fans restart when the drip time of 4 min has passed.
