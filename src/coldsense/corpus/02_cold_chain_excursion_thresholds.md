# KB-CR-102: Commercial Refrigeration Excursion & Cold-Chain Thresholds

## Section 2.1: Critical Temperature Thresholds and Holdover Limits
Chilled storage cold rooms must maintain internal air temperature between 0.0 C and 4.0 C during normal compressor cycling. The critical excursion threshold is 8.0 C. If internal air temperature exceeds 8.0 C for more than 120 minutes during a power cut, quality assurance quarantine protocols must be initiated and core product temperature probes must be verified within 15 minutes.

## Section 2.2: Pharmaceutical and Perishable Holdover Classification
During an unpowered drift event, the holdover time is defined as the elapsed duration from power loss (t0) until the 95% upper confidence bound of the first-order thermal model crosses 8.0 C. If holdover time is less than 60 minutes, technicians must keep cold-room doors strictly closed and verify backup generator transfer status before opening any access panels.
