# KB-CR-101: Cold-Room Thermal Envelope & Door Gasket Maintenance Manual

## Section 4.1: Passive Thermal Drift & Insulation Standards
A properly insulated commercial walk-in cold room operating at a nominal setpoint of 2.0 C should exhibit a passive first-order thermal time constant tau between 3.5 hours and 6.0 hours during an unpowered drift test at 22.0 C ambient temperature. If the fitted thermal time constant tau falls below 2.5 hours, technicians must inspect the cold-room envelope for Polyurethane (PU) panel joint separation, unsealed electrical conduit penetrations, or moisture-saturated insulation cores.

## Section 4.2: Door Perimeter Seal & Paper-Drag Inspection Procedure
When Perform Perimeter Door Gasket inspection is required, technicians must use a 0.1 mm feeler gauge or standard paper strip every 30 cm around the full door perimeter. Any perimeter gap exceeding 1.5 mm, torn magnetic vinyl elastomer, or localized frost trail requires immediate door gasket replacement. Automatic door closers must latch firmly from a 15 cm opening angle within 4 seconds.
