# CU-40 Condensing Unit Installation Sheet

> SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Fictional manufacturer ("Testline", invented for ColdSense tests), fictional product
> and invented values. Not guidance for any real equipment.

## Location

Install the unit outdoors with at least 300 mm of free space on each side. Keep it out of direct sunlight.

## Electrical connection

The CU-40 needs a 400 V, 3-phase, 50 Hz supply protected by a 16 A fuse.

## Refrigerant

The unit uses R-454C with a factory charge of 1.2 kg. Do not add refrigerant unless the line set is longer than
10 m.

## High pressure switch

The high pressure switch opens at 28 bar and resets automatically at 22 bar. It must never be bypassed or
bridged, not even for a test.

## Condenser fan

The condenser fan starts when the condensing pressure rises above 14 bar.
