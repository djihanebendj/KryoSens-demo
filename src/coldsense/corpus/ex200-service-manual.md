# EX-200 Walk-in Cooler Service Manual

> SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Fictional manufacturer ("Exemplar Refrigeration", invented for ColdSense tests),
> fictional product and invented values. Not guidance for any real equipment.

## 1 Safety

Disconnect the electrical supply before opening any panel. Only qualified refrigeration technicians may work on
the refrigerant circuit.

## 2 Specifications

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Operating range | +2 to +8 °C |
| Refrigerant | R-290, charge 150 g |
| Supply | 230 V, 50 Hz |
| Maximum current | 6.5 A |

## 3 Controller

The factory default setpoint is +4 °C with a differential of 2 K. To change the setpoint, hold the SET key for
3 s, adjust with the arrow keys and confirm with SET.

## 4 Defrost

### 4.1 Defrost type

The EX-200 uses electric defrost with a heater on the evaporator coil.

### 4.2 Defrost interval

The defrost interval is 6 h. A defrost cycle lasts at most 25 min and terminates when the evaporator probe
reaches 8 °C.

## 5 Alarms

### 5.1 High-temperature alarm

The high-temperature alarm triggers when the room temperature stays above the setpoint plus 5 K for 60 min.

### 5.2 Door alarm

The door alarm sounds when the door stays open for more than 3 min.

## 6 Maintenance

Clean the condenser coil every 3 months. Inspect the door gasket every month and replace it if it is torn.

## 7 Room probe

Install the room probe at 1.5 m height, away from the evaporator air outlet and the door. Check the probe against a
calibrated reference thermometer once a year; the tolerance is 0.5 °C.
